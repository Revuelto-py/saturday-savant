How it works: completed results are fixed, and every remaining game through selection day is decided by its Savant Forecast win probability. Conference title games pair the top two teams by conference record. Selection and seeding follow the committee's 2026 protocol: the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions and the highest-ranked team from the American, C-USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 and Sun Belt get automatic bids, Notre Dame is in if ranked in the top 12, the next highest-ranked teams fill the field, the top four ranked teams get the byes, and an automatic qualifier ranked outside the top 12 is seeded last. The committee's ranking itself is a stand-in: Savant Rating, plus 3.5 points per game over .500, plus half the strength of schedule, with head-to-head results deciding between near-equal teams. Playoff games use the Savant Rating gap. For the field as the rankings stand today, see the playoff bracket.
