This is the field the current AP poll and results would produce under the selection committee's 2026 protocol: the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions get automatic bids, as does the highest-ranked team from the other six conferences, Notre Dame is in if it is ranked in the top 12, and the next highest-ranked teams fill the field. Seeding follows the ranking, so the top four ranked teams get the byes, and an automatic qualifier ranked outside the top 12 is seeded last. No games are simulated, so the first round is filled in and every round after it stays open.
